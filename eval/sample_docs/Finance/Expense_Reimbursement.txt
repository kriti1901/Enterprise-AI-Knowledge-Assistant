Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees must submit expense claims within 30 days of the purchase date. Claims submitted after 30 days are rejected unless the Finance Director approves an exception in writing.

Travel expenses are reimbursed at the following rates: economy class flights in full with prior approval, hotels up to 180 USD per night in standard cities and 250 USD per night in premium cities, and meals up to 60 USD per day.

Every claim above 100 USD requires an itemized receipt. Personal entertainment is never reimbursable.

Reimbursements are paid in the next monthly payroll cycle after approval.
